About
me.

I’m Anna, a freelance designer for brand and communication design based in Munich, Germany.

I develop brands that people don’t just see, but understand. For me, everything starts with the idea. Then I follow it through consistently: from logo, packaging and campaign into physical space and onto the screen. I love working with small businesses and start-ups that dare to try something new and are looking for clear, bold design. I also use new tools like AI image generation to make ideas visible quickly.

Graduate of AMD Akademie Mode & Design in Munich. Agency experience at KMS TEAM in Munich.
